The Lab

Build it, and it takes shape

Tap atoms onto the canvas, or type a name, a formula or a SMILES string. Hydrogens fill in, the structure is checked as you build, and the molecule settles into its 3D geometry in real time.

  • Live 3DBall-and-stick, licorice, space-filling or wireframe. Turn it, zoom it, measure any distance or angle, and see the Lewis structure beside it.

  • VSEPR, explainedThe shape, its AXE notation and every bond angle, with the lone pairs shown where they push the bonds apart.

  • Polarity and forcesBond dipoles and the overall dipole, a polar or nonpolar verdict with the reason, and the intermolecular forces that follow.

  • 86 molecules to start fromFrom water and ammonia to caffeine, aspirin and glucose, each with where you meet it, properties and sources. Compare two side by side.

All 118 elements, down to the electrons

Every element with its properties, electron configuration, uses, discovery and sources. Color the table by periodic trends to see why radius shrinks across a period and ionization energy climbs.

Open the Orbital Explorer to see s, p, d and f orbitals in 3D, build an electron configuration box by box, and read molecular-orbital diagrams and photoelectron spectra.

Reaction Lab

Reactions you can follow step by step

Twenty-four curated reactions, from combustion and neutralization to esterification, each with a balanced equation, conditions, an energy diagram showing activation energy and ΔH, and what to notice.

Mechanisms play one step at a time with curved arrows and the 3D structures moving between them: SN2 substitution, electrophilic addition and Fischer esterification. Stuck on a balance? Send the equation to the balancer and see the coefficients worked out.

Questions

Is Chemistry 3D free?

Yes. The Lab, live 3D, VSEPR and polarity, the full periodic table, the simulations, the calculators, most lessons and the glossary are free, with 10 saved projects and 10 AI explanations a day. Chemistry 3D Pro unlocks everything else.

Is there a free trial?

No. There is no free trial. The free version stays free with no time limit, so you can try the app properly before you decide on Pro.

Do I need an account? What data do you collect?

No account. Chemistry 3D collects no data: no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and the app talks to no server of ours. Your projects stay on your device, or in your own private iCloud if you turn iCloud sync on.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The chemistry engine, the library, the periodic table, simulations, calculators, lessons and the on-device AI all work without a connection. iCloud sync and buying or restoring Pro need the network.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, Mac with macOS 26, Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 26, and Apple Watch with watchOS 26 as a companion app with complications. One Pro purchase covers all of them with the same Apple Account.

Which languages are included?

20: English, Tiếng Việt, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Русский, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文, 繁體中文, العربية, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia, ไทย, Türkçe, Nederlands, Polski and Українська. The app starts in US English; choose another language in Settings › Language.

How do the AI explanations work?

On devices with Apple Intelligence, explanations are written on the device from the numbers the chemistry engine calculated, and nothing you ask leaves the device. Where Apple Intelligence is not available, the app shows curated explanations instead.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Cancel in your Apple Account subscriptions at least 24 hours before the period ends. To restore, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.
