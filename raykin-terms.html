6. Clean Share

Clean Share removes identifying information, such as names, IDs, birth dates and hospital details, from the DICOM data in a copy, shifts the dates, and flags text that may be burned into the images. Identifying details can still appear in ways software cannot always detect, such as text inside an image. You must review everything you share before you send it, and you are responsible for what you share.
