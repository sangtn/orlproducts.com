Car.TV Support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Write to info@orlproducts.com, or in the app use Settings › Contact support.

Try Car.TV in one tap

Car.TV comes with no channels. To see every feature right away, open Settings › Sample playlist › Add sample playlist. It contains only public test streams (Apple, Unified Streaming, Mux) and Blender open movies, and does not count toward the free plan’s one source.

Sample playlist (M3U)

Add your own playlist or server

  1. Tap Add a source on Home, or Settings › Sources › Add.
  2. Choose the type: an M3U/M3U8 link, a file, a QR code, Xtream Codes, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby or a WebDAV share.
  3. Paste the link your provider gave you (and an XMLTV guide link if you have one), then tap Test & add.

Play on CarPlay

  1. Connect your iPhone to the car with a cable, or wirelessly if your car supports it, and allow CarPlay when your iPhone asks.
  2. On the car screen, open Car.TV.
  3. Pick a channel or film from Home, Live TV, Movies or Series. The sound plays through the car speakers and the steering-wheel buttons control it.

Show iPhone on the car display

For passengers, or when the car is parked. Never watch while driving.

  1. Connect to CarPlay and open Car.TV on the car screen.
  2. Tap I’m a passenger on the car screen to confirm.
  3. On your iPhone, open Car.TV › Settings › Show iPhone on car display and tap Start sharing screen, then Start Broadcast.
  4. Your iPhone screen now appears on the car display. Stop it from the same button or the red indicator in the status bar.

Apps that protect their video, such as most paid streaming services, show black when shared. That is iOS, on purpose.

Cast to a TV

In the player, tap the cast button at the top right (it appears as soon as a TV is found on your Wi-Fi). Car.TV lists AirPlay devices, Chromecast and Google TV, and DLNA smart TVs. If none appear, check the TV is on, on the same Wi-Fi, and that Local Network is allowed for Car.TV in Settings.

A channel won’t play

  • Test the same link in another player; if it fails there too, the provider is down or the link has expired.
  • Some formats (for example MKV with DTS audio) can’t be decoded by iOS. Car.TV marks them “Phone only” or says so instead of showing a black screen.
  • Settings › Diagnostics shows what the last attempt returned; include it when you write to us.
