# Car.TV — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Car.TV is made by DeeplyAi. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Car.TV on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and CarPlay. The short version: Car.TV does not collect your data. We have no servers that receive it, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

## What stays on your device
The playlists, servers and video files you add, your favourites, watch progress and settings are stored on your device. Passwords and access tokens for the servers you add are kept in the iOS Keychain.

## iCloud sync (optional)
If you turn on iCloud sync, your library and progress are stored in your own private iCloud database so your devices match. Apple holds that data under your Apple Account; DeeplyAi cannot read it. Passwords are never synced.

## Who Car.TV talks to
Car.TV connects only to the playlists, portals and media servers you add yourself, to fetch the channels and videos you choose. Those services have their own privacy policies. Car.TV also contacts the App Store to show prices and process purchases, which Apple handles.

## Local network
To play videos on a Chromecast, Google TV or smart TV, Car.TV looks for those devices on your Wi-Fi and, when you cast a video stored on this iPhone, serves it to that TV. Nothing leaves your local network for this.

## Camera and photos
The camera is used only to scan a playlist QR code when you ask it to; images are not stored. Photo library access is used only to play videos you choose.

## Purchases
Purchases are handled by Apple. Car.TV receives only whether a purchase is active, never your payment details.

## Children
Car.TV is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone. The parental lock can hide adult categories.

## Tracking
Car.TV does not track you across apps or websites and does not share data with data brokers.
