Open what the hospital gave you

Hospitals hand out scans in all sorts of ways. Raykin opens them and sorts everything into people and visits for you.

CD or DVD

On a Mac, insert the disc and Raykin copies it first, then you can eject it. It never runs the viewer programs on the disc.

USB-C drive

Plug the drive into your iPhone, iPad or Mac and choose it in Raykin.

Files, folders and ZIP

Open single DICOM files, whole folders or a ZIP archive.

DICOMDIR

Many hospital discs include a DICOMDIR index file, and Raykin reads it.

Hospital QR code

Scan the code on your results paper. Raykin opens the hospital’s page and captures the downloads automatically.

Portal link

Open the link the hospital sent you. Downloads are captured automatically and filed for you.

Photo of an old film

No disc for an old X-ray film? Photograph it against a bright light and keep it with the rest.

Paper report

Scan a printed report with the camera and keep it with your scans.

A viewer that explains itself

Everything you need to look closely, with plain-language help whenever a word or a series name is unfamiliar.

  • Scroll through slices, and play moving images as a loop.
  • Adjust brightness and contrast, or pick a preset.
  • Zoom, rotate and invert.
  • View one to four images at a time.
  • Cross-reference lines and a localizer mini-map show where each slice sits.
  • The Series Explainer says what an MRI sequence or CT series is for, in plain words, and a glossary covers the rest.
  • Star key images so you can find them again.
  • See the views and marks the hospital saved with your scan.

When you need to look closer

Raykin Pro adds tools for comparing, measuring, exploring in 3D and sharing safely. You can try every one of them free on the built-in sample family.

Then & Now

Put two dates side by side to see what has changed. Flicker between them or blend one over the other, and positions stay in sync as you scroll.

Measurements

Measure distances in millimetres, angles and areas, and probe CT density in HU. Measurements are approximate.

Raykin doesn’t interpret differences. Ask your doctor what they mean.

MPR & 3D

See a CT or MRI series from every side: axial, coronal and sagittal, oblique planes, and thick slabs with MIP, MinIP or average.

3D volume rendering

Presets for bone, skin, vessels, lung and more. 3D views are approximate and for viewing only.

Clean Share

Sending scans for a second opinion? Clean Share makes a copy with the personal details taken out, so you share the images and not your identity.

  1. Removes names, IDs, birth dates and hospital details from the copy.
  2. Shifts the dates.
  3. Flags text burned into the images, so you can cover it.
  4. Shares a ZIP, images, a video or a second-opinion PDF packet.

Your originals never change. Always look over what you share before you send it.

Questions

Is Raykin a medical device?

No. Raykin is for viewing and organising your own medical images. It is not intended for diagnosis, treatment or clinical decisions, and it does not replace your doctor or the hospital’s official report.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Raykin has no account and no servers. Your scans stay in the app’s private storage on your device, and nothing is sent to us.

I have a CD, but no CD drive.

Use Raykin on a Mac with a disc drive, ask the hospital for a USB drive, ZIP file or download link, or copy the disc to a USB drive on any computer.

Can I try Raykin Pro first?

Yes. Every Pro tool works free on the built-in sample family, so you can see how it works before you buy. There is no free trial on your own scans.
