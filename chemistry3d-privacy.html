Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

In short: Chemistry 3D does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app does not talk to any server of ours. On the App Store its privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

Everything you make and do in Chemistry 3D is stored on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro or Apple Watch: molecules and projects with their versions and notes, lesson progress, calculator history, favorites, recent items and settings. The chemistry engine, the molecule library, the periodic table, the simulations and the calculators all run on the device. Nothing is sent to us, and we have no way to see it.

Deleting the app deletes this data from that device. In the app, Settings › Delete All Data removes every project from the device and, if iCloud sync is on, from your iCloud as well.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is turned on, your projects sync between your devices through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), and lesson progress through your iCloud key-value storage. This data lives in your Apple Account under Apple’s iCloud terms; only you can read it, and ORIENTPRO cannot access it. You can turn sync off in Settings › iCloud sync; the app then keeps everything on the device only.

AI explanations

On devices with Apple Intelligence, explanations are written on the device by Apple’s on-device model, using only the numbers the chemistry engine calculated. What you ask is not sent to us or to anyone else. Where Apple Intelligence is not available or is turned off, the app shows curated explanations that are built into the app. You can turn AI explanations off in Settings › Apple Intelligence.

Permissions

Chemistry 3D asks for one permission, and only when you use it:

  • Photos, add only: so the app can save an image you choose to export. It cannot see, read or browse your photo library.

Chemistry 3D does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendars or health data, and it does not ask for them.

Files, exports and sharing

Exports (images, PDF study sheets, SVG, SMILES, MOL, SDF, XYZ, PDB, USDZ and CSV files) are created on the device and go only where you send them, through the system share sheet or Files. Files you open in the app (SMILES, MOL, SDF, XYZ, PDB and Chemistry 3D projects) are read on the device. “Report an error” and “Contact Support” open your own Mail app with a message you can review; the app sends nothing by itself.

Apple Watch and widgets

The Apple Watch app, its complications and the Home Screen widgets show content from the app on your devices, such as the Element of the Day, the Molecule of the Day and the name of the project you were working on. They collect nothing and send nothing to us.

Purchases

Chemistry 3D Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. The app checks your purchases on the device with Apple’s StoreKit and never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple’s privacy policy covers that transaction.

Diagnostics

Chemistry 3D contains no analytics or crash-reporting code of its own. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports on to us. You can change this at any time in your device’s Privacy & Security settings.

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to answer you, we do not add you to any list, and we do not share it.

Children and students

Chemistry 3D is made for learners, including school students. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children. There is no account, no chat, no advertising and no way to contact other users in the app.

Your choices

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Everything is under your control on your devices and in your iCloud: turn iCloud sync or AI explanations off in Settings, use Delete All Data, or delete the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. A change that would collect any data would also be shown in the app and on the App Store privacy label before it takes effect.

Questions: write to info@orlproducts.com and a person will answer. Chemistry 3D is published by ORIENTPRO – LA INVESTMENT & TRADE., JSC.
