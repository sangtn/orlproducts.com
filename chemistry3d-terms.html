5. Educational use and accuracy

Chemistry 3D is an educational tool. Values in the app are labeled as Reference (curated data with its source), Calculated (computed by the app’s chemistry engine), Model (a teaching model with known limits) or Illustrative. 3D geometries are models generated on the device, and AI explanations are generated text built from those values. We work hard to make everything correct, but mistakes are possible: check any result that matters, such as an exam answer, lab work or published material, against your course materials or a primary source. If you find an error, please tell us.
