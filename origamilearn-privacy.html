Privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026

What this app collects

Nothing. Origami Learn has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We do not know who you are and we cannot see what you fold.

What stays on your device

Your progress, your chosen colours, your gallery photos and your settings are written to this app's own storage on this device. Photos you add come from the system photo picker, which hands the app one picture and no access to your library. If you switch on voice control, the microphone is open only while a model is on screen, the words are recognised on this device, and the audio is never recorded, kept or sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

If you buy Plus, Apple handles the payment. We receive no card details and no name — only whether this Apple Account is entitled. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Children

The app is safe for children: there is nothing to collect, the only links out go to the App Store, to Apple's licence agreement and to our support email, and Kids Mode hides models that need scissors and puts purchases behind a grown-ups check. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to ask a parent's consent for.

Questions

Write to info@orlproducts.com and a person will answer.
