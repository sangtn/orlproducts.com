OmniDoc: Anything to PDFiPhone · iPad · Mac · Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how OmniDoc: Anything to PDF (“OmniDoc”), made by ORIENTPRO – LA INVESTMENT & TRADE., JSC (“ORIENTPRO”, “we”), handles information on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. OmniDoc turns scans, photos, PDF, Office, iWork and text files, recordings, audio files and the audio of video files into searchable PDFs, transcripts, meeting minutes and study notes, and lets you edit, sign and print PDFs.

The short version

  • OmniDoc does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK in the app.
  • We have no servers. We never receive your files, recordings, transcripts, documents or anything else.
  • Text recognition, transcription and AI documents are all processed on your device. Your files stay on your device.
  • iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your documents sync only through your own private iCloud, which we cannot access.

1. What we collect

Nothing. OmniDoc’s App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected”. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information, and there is nothing to sign in to.

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell or share personal information. OmniDoc contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK and no other third-party SDK.

2. Processing on your device

The work OmniDoc does happens on your own device, using Apple’s built-in frameworks:

  • Text recognition in scans, photos and images uses Apple’s Vision framework, on device.
  • Speech transcription of recordings, audio files and the audio of video files uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition.
  • AI documents such as meeting minutes and study notes use the on-device Apple Intelligence model, on devices that support Apple Intelligence.
  • Conversion, PDF editing, signatures and search are done by OmniDoc itself, on device.

Your files, recordings, transcripts and documents stay on your device, in OmniDoc’s own storage. OmniDoc’s widgets, Live Activity and Siri and Shortcuts actions read that same on-device data and do not send it anywhere.

If you have iCloud Backup turned on, your device backup may include OmniDoc’s data, the same as for any other app. That backup is between you and Apple.

3. iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings › iCloud Sync, your documents sync between your devices only through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). That database belongs to your Apple Account; we cannot access it and receive nothing from it. You can turn sync off again at any time.

OmniDoc also uses iCloud key-value storage, in your own iCloud account, for one thing only: a small counter of how many free AI documents you have used, so the free allowance is the same on all your devices. It contains none of your content.

iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Import from Link

When you paste a link into Import from Link, OmniDoc downloads that one document directly from that website to your device. The website sees a normal download request, as it would from your browser, including your IP address. Nothing is sent to us, and OmniDoc does not browse the web or contact any other site.

5. Permissions

Every permission is optional. Each one is asked for only when you first use the feature that needs it, and you can change it at any time in your device’s Settings.

  • Camera — to scan documents and to photograph a signature.
  • Microphone — to record meetings, lectures and notes.
  • Speech recognition — to make transcripts.
  • Notifications — to tell you when a document is ready. They are scheduled on your device; there is no push server.
  • Face ID — only if you turn on App Lock, to unlock OmniDoc. Apple handles the check; OmniDoc never sees your face data.
  • Local network — to find AirPrint printers on your network. When you print, the document goes from your device to your printer.

If you decline a permission, everything that does not need it keeps working.

6. Apple Watch and Mac

Recordings made on Apple Watch are sent directly to your paired iPhone, where they appear in your Library. The Mac app, including its menu bar recorder, keeps everything on your Mac in the same way. None of this passes through us.

7. Contacting support

When you choose Contact Support, OmniDoc opens your own mail app with a new message to us. Nothing is sent until you send it. Diagnostics (the app version, device model, system version and the status of recent processing jobs) are attached only if you choose to include them, and they are shown to you before you choose. We use what you send only to answer you.

8. Purchases

OmniDoc Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your payment details, your Apple Account or your billing address. The app only asks Apple whether a valid purchase exists. Restore Purchases asks Apple the same question again; it does not create an account and does not identify you to us.

9. Children

OmniDoc is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

10. Your rights under GDPR, CCPA and similar laws

These laws give you the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none. There is no account to close and no record of you to produce, correct or erase.

11. Deleting your data

Your data is on your devices and under your control. Delete a document in OmniDoc, or delete the app, and it is gone from that device. If you turned on iCloud sync, a document you delete also moves to Recently Deleted on your other devices, and deleting it permanently removes it from your iCloud. You can also manage OmniDoc’s iCloud data in your device’s iCloud settings.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. If a change ever means OmniDoc starts collecting something, we will say so plainly.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to info@orlproducts.com.
