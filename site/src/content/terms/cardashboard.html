3. Speed, speed limits and alerts

Speed comes from GPS or from your car through an OBD-II adapter and can be inaccurate, especially in tunnels, among tall buildings or with a weak signal. Speed limits come from OpenStreetMap data and can be missing, outdated or wrong. Road signs and the instructions of the police always take precedence over what the app shows. Overspeed alerts are a reminder, not a guarantee; drive within the limit whether or not an alert sounds.

4. Speed camera alerts where legal

Camera alerts are off until you turn them on. They use fixed camera positions from OpenStreetMap contributors, which may be incomplete, and never radar detection or reports of police checks. Local laws decide whether camera alerts may be used. CarDashboard adjusts automatically by country (for example, only danger zones in France, no alerts while driving in Germany and none in Switzerland), but border areas and the rules themselves can change, and you are responsible for following the law where you drive. CarDashboard is meant to help you drive within the limit, not to avoid enforcement.

5. Crash detection and SOS

Crash detection can miss a crash or detect one by mistake. It does not replace emergency services, and it depends on your device's sensors, battery, signal and settings. Messages and calls go through your own phone carrier and may fail. The app never calls emergency services by itself; in an emergency, call them yourself. Use the test function to check your setup, and tell your emergency contacts that they may receive messages from you.

6. OBD-II diagnostics

CarDashboard reads standard diagnostic data from your car through an OBD-II adapter you choose. It never changes vehicle settings. Clearing trouble codes (Pro) turns off the check-engine light and resets readiness monitors; only do it when you understand the consequences, and never as a substitute for a repair. Adapters and vehicles differ, values can be wrong, and some cars do not support every reading. Consult a qualified mechanic before acting on a trouble code.
