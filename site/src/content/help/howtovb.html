How to import music from browser?

1. Click the "Add Music" button.
2. Select "From Browser".
3. Go to a music website (e.g., YouTube).
4. Play the song or playlist you want.
5. A playlist button will appear in the top-right corner of the browser's address bar.
6. Click the playlist button and add the music to your playlist to enjoy it.

How to import music from Apple Music?

1. Click the "Add Music" button.
2. Select "From Apple Music".
3. Allow access when prompted.
4. Choose your desired song and click "Done" to confirm.

How to import music from Files app/ Photo Albums?

1. Click the "Add Music" button.
2. Choose either:
- "From Files" (to select a local file), or
- "From Photos" (to pick a song from your photo library).
3. If using "From Photos", grant permission by selecting "Allow Access".
4. Browse and select your desired song.
5. Tap "Done" to confirm.
