In short: CarDashboard has no account and no server that receives your data. Your location, trips, vehicles, contacts and photos stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob on parked screens only, never while you drive; Google's ads software collects some device data to show and measure those ads. Section 9 explains that and how to choose. Pro users see no ads, and the ads software is not started for them.

1. Who we are

CarDashboard (package com.orlproducts.cardashboard) is published by ORIENTPRO – LA INVESTMENT & TRADE., JSC (“ORIENTPRO”, “we”). This policy applies to the app on Android phones, tablets and Android car head units. You can reach us at info@orlproducts.com.

2. Data we collect

We, ORIENTPRO, collect none. CarDashboard has no account, does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and has no analytics. We do not receive your location, speed, trips, vehicles, parking spots, engine data, contacts or settings. The only third-party software in the app that collects data is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) with Google's User Messaging Platform, for free users (section 9), and Google Play for purchases (section 10).

3. Data stored on your device

Trips (time, distance, speeds, a simplified route line and overspeed counts), vehicle profiles, parking spots and their optional photo and note, saved places (Home, Work, recent destinations), speed-dial and emergency contacts you picked, engine trouble codes, imported sounds, themes, layouts and settings are stored only on your device. You can delete any trip, vehicle or contact in the app, export everything to a file you keep (Settings › Privacy › Export my data), or remove everything with Settings › Privacy › Delete all my data.

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may back up CarDashboard's settings, layouts and themes to your Google account. Trips, map packs and sounds are excluded from cloud backup. That backup is handled by Google under your account and we cannot read it.

4. Location

CarDashboard asks for location while the app is in use so it can show your speed, the speed limit, the map and record trips. Location is processed on your device and never sent to us. The app does not request background location: trip recording runs in a visible notification that you can stop at any time. The country you are in is worked out on your device from a built-in map of borders, to apply the right speed-camera rules. You can use CarDashboard without location, but speed, limits and trips then need an OBD-II adapter.

5. Services the app connects to

  • Weather — MET Norway (api.met.no). To show the weather, the app sends your position rounded to about 1 km (two decimal places) and an app identifier. No other data is sent. MET Norway's data is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • Map tiles — OpenFreeMap. The map downloads tiles for the area on screen. Like any website, the tile server sees your IP address and which tiles are requested. Viewed tiles are cached on your device.
  • Map packs — packs.orlproducts.com. Offline speed limits, camera positions and nearby fuel and parking come from free packs per country or state, built from OpenStreetMap. Downloading a pack sends only the name of the pack you chose. The server is static file hosting; it keeps no account and receives no location.
  • Navigation apps. When you tap Navigate, CarDashboard opens Google Maps, Waze or another navigation app you choose, with the destination. That app's own privacy policy applies from then on.

6. Permissions

  • Location (while in use) — speed, speed limit, map, trips, parking spot.
  • Notifications — the trip notification, the crash countdown and the parking-meter reminder.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth) — to connect an OBD-II adapter and to recognise your car's Bluetooth for the startup sound and auto-start. Car association uses Android's companion-device system; CarDashboard does not use Bluetooth to find your location.
  • Display over other apps — only for the floating speed bubble over your navigation app, if you turn it on.
  • Phone calls — to call a speed-dial contact in one tap and, if you set up SOS, to call your primary emergency contact.
  • Notification access (optional) — to show the title and artwork of what is playing and control it. CarDashboard reads only the active media session; it does not read, store or send your notifications.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional, Pro) — to turn on Driving Do Not Disturb when your car connects.

CarDashboard never asks for access to your contacts list, call log, text messages, storage or camera. Contacts are picked one at a time with Android's contact picker, and only the name and number you picked are kept. Parking photos are taken by your camera app and saved in CarDashboard's private storage.

7. Crash detection and SOS

Crash detection is off until you set it up. While you drive, it uses the motion sensors on your device; this data is analysed on the device and not stored, except a short record of a detected event (time, force, speed before and after) in your trip history. If you don't cancel the countdown, CarDashboard opens a text message with your location for your emergency contacts and calls your primary contact. These go through your own phone carrier, not through us. Your emergency contacts and your name stay on your device.

8. OBD-II engine data

If you connect an OBD-II adapter, engine values (such as speed, engine speed, temperatures and battery voltage), trouble codes and your vehicle identification number are read on your device and kept there. CarDashboard only reads diagnostic data; it never changes vehicle settings. Clearing trouble codes (Pro) is the only command sent to the car, and only when you confirm it.

9. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob on parked screens only (for example Home, Trips and Settings). There are no ads in Drive Mode, the speed bubble, the HUD, widgets, Quick Settings tiles, notifications or while the vehicle is moving. There are no full-screen ads when the app opens.

Before any ad is requested in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, Google's consent message asks for your choice. You can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy › Privacy & ad choices. Google's ads software may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address and information about how ads are shown and interacted with, to show, measure and protect ads. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. CarDashboard Pro removes all ads, and the ads software is not started for Pro users.

An optional rewarded ad lets you try one Pro theme for 24 hours. Watching it is always your choice.

10. Purchases

The app checks your purchase with Google Play on your device to unlock Pro. We have no server that stores purchases.

11. Children

CarDashboard is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Security and retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it, delete all data in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. All network connections use HTTPS, except the local Wi-Fi connection to an OBD-II adapter inside your car. Map packs are checked against a signed list before they are installed.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For data held by Google (ads, purchases, backup), use your Google account settings. Our company-wide privacy statement is at orlproducts.com/privacy.html.
